Frequently Asked Questions

How does SmartRide AI compare fares across Uber, Ola, Rapido and Namma Yatri?

Our intelligent EMMDE Multi-Agent Engine monitors real-time telemetry, surge multipliers, traffic congestion, and distance matrices across all top mobility providers in your area, giving you an apples-to-apples price and ETA comparison.

What should I do if a driver cancels my trip?

SmartRide features automated Zero-Cancellation fallback: if an assigned driver cancels, our system automatically shifts your request to the next highest-scoring provider with zero cancellation penalty fees.

How does Smart Commute carpooling safety work?

Every commuter in the Smart Commute network is verified through corporate email verification, government ID checks, and real-time route deviation monitoring. You can also select the 'Women Only Pool' filter for dedicated safe carpooling.

What package items can be sent using Parcel Express?

You can send business documents, electronics, keys, boxed retail packages, books, and food. Heavy items up to 50 kg can be delivered using our mini-truck fleet. Hazardous or prohibited goods are not permitted.
